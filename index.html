Wood Finish

The Right Finish
for the Right Piece

Every Hazy's Wood piece receives a hand-applied finish selected for the specific timber, intended use, and environment. Here's what goes into the pieces you love.

🔬
Penetrating Oil
Deep-penetrating natural oils bond with the wood fibres at a molecular level — not just a surface coat. Enhances grain and protects from within.
🛡️
Hard Wax Topcoat
Premium hard wax creates a water-repellent surface barrier that protects against moisture, UV, and daily wear without plasticising the wood.
🍴
Food-Safe Oils
All boards, pens, and kitchen pieces are finished exclusively with 100% food-safe, zero-VOC oils. Safe for direct food contact from day one.
✨
Resin Seal & Polish
River tables receive additional epoxy sealing and hand-polished buffing for a glass-like surface that brings the resin river to life.

FAQ

Common Questions
Straight Answers

An epoxy river table is made by splitting a live-edge timber slab in two and pouring artist-grade epoxy resin between the two halves — creating the appearance of a flowing river through the wood. At Hazy's Wood, we use premium Australian hardwoods and UV-stable, bubble-free deep-pour epoxy. The process involves sealing the timber, building a mould, mixing and pouring the resin, curing, sanding through multiple grits, and finishing with food-safe penetrating oil and hard wax. Each piece takes 4–8 weeks to complete.
Hazy's Wood uses premium Australian burls, exotic imported timbers including Purpleheart, Padauk, Bocote, and Amboyna, plus custom-poured epoxy resin blanks. Each pen blank is individually selected for grain pattern, figure, and character before being precision-lathed and polished. All pens are fitted with high-quality hardware and available in fountain pen, rollerball, and twist-action styles.
