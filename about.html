Every piece of wood is a masterpiece waiting to emerge.

Hi, I'm Hazy — the craftsman, creator, and podcast host behind Hazy's Wood. Based in Sydney, I've spent years turning raw Australian timber into functional art that people cherish for a lifetime.

What started as a passion for natural materials grew into a full creative practice — from live edge river tables and resin art, to personalised boards and jewellery boxes. Every commission is a collaboration, and every piece carries the unique character of the timber it came from.

I believe the best things are made by hand, with intention, using materials that carry history. That philosophy drives everything I build.

When I'm not in the workshop, I'm co-hosting The Timber Bros Podcast — conversations about woodworking, making, and the craft business in Australia.
